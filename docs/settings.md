# Settings and the profile

## The board's settings

The Mac board's settings (the background colour, the switches on the settings page, the chime's mute and where the board's boxes sit) belong to the board, not to one browser, so another browser, or the board after a port move, shows them the same. Each window keeps its own open card and project. The phone keeps its own settings, apart from the two it shares with the Mac (see [The settings page](#the-settings-page)).

## The settings page

On the Mac board, the squid at the right end of the top bar opens the settings page over the board; the red button at its top left, Escape, or a click outside closes it. Its sections, in this order:

- Profile: a picture (one of twelve small animal, face and object drawings, with no name shown, drawn at random the first time Settings asks on this Mac, then kept in the profile for good and never sent anywhere; nothing to click yet) with Name and Email beside it, then Work and your time zone, written out as in "Pacific Time, UTC−7". The email is the yes to feedback emails and there is no switch for it (see [Usage counts](privacy.md#usage-counts)).
- Appearance: the background colour, and "Cards waiting on you", which lists the cards waiting on you newest first instead of oldest first.
- Editor: "Format text while typing", off unless you turn it on.
- Improvements: "Share Feedback", with the line "Encountered bugs? Have feature requests or questions? Please tell us how we can improve." and a feedback box under it, above "Share daily usage counts", which is on unless you turn it off (see [Usage counts](privacy.md#usage-counts)). The box is greyed out until your Profile has a name and an email; type a message, press the arrow (or Enter) and you have three seconds to press the cross and cancel before it is sent with your name and email, and "Sent. Thank you." shows under the box. The bug in the top bar opens this same box, alone, in the middle of the window over the same dark shade as Settings: it holds the title, the line and the box and nothing else, and a click anywhere outside it (or Escape) puts it away. It is the one box, so closing it keeps what you typed for next time and a send already counting down carries on.
- Bin: the cards the garbage can beside the tickets deleted, newest first, each with its title, its project and when it was deleted, and a restore icon (an arrow curling back, titled Restore) that shows at the row's right end while the pointer is on or near the row and puts the card back in its own place and section, whole. A card is deleted for good after 30 days here, and nothing empties the Bin sooner.

On the phone the settings are in a drawer, and only the squid on the bottom row opens it: no pull and no key does. A pull from the right edge brings a card's magic box, and on a card without one it does nothing. The drawer's sections are Profile, Appearance, Editor, Notifications and Improvements; there is no Bin, since only the Mac restores deleted cards. Improvements holds the same feedback box (it is sent by the Mac) above "Share daily usage counts"; the phone has no bug in its bottom row, and its box is set at 16px so that tapping it does not zoom the page in. "Cards waiting on you" and "Share daily usage counts" (with the same caption) are shared with the Mac; the profile is only shown, since it is changed on the Mac: its picture, name, email, work and the time zone in plain words, with no feedback switch. The power mark at the bottom of the drawer signs the phone out. A tap beside the drawer, or a swipe to the right, puts it away.

A new install starts with a white background and plain typing. To change those starting points wherever nobody has chosen, set `"background_default"` (a colour such as `"#ffffff"`) or `"compose_format_default": true` in `run.config.json` and run `facilitator restart`. A choice made on the settings page always wins.

## The onboarding and the profile

Each Mac board asks a few questions once, the first time it opens (or on an older board with no profile yet), and they cover the board until they are done. The phone is never blocked by them.

It is a run of real cards: Welcome (the Facilitator mark as its header, then "Hi there 👋", one line about the app and the right arrow for the next card), one card titled "Let's onboard you onto the Facilitator!", four short cards: 101: Atomizing asks, 101: Tickets, 101: Hotkeys and 101: Phone App (a picture slot, then how to set up the phone app with `facilitator bridge setup`), and a last card titled "You are all set up" that thanks you and asks for bugs and feedback through the bug button in the top-right corner. Right arrow, or Control + Shift + right while typing, goes to the next card. The cards after the onboarding card open only once every question is answered: a move to them before that, by any key or click, stays on the onboarding card and shows the current question's short line. The ticket list beside the card stays hidden until 101: Tickets, then shows and stays.

The onboarding card asks its questions one after another as its replies: What should we call you?, What's your email? and What will you mostly use Facilitator for?. Tab goes to the reply box and Enter sends; each answer brings the next question. Every question must be answered: Enter with the box empty keeps the question and shows one short line asking for its answer, and so does an email with no @ or no dot after it. Giving an email is saying yes to feedback emails. The time zone is read from the Mac. The last reply lists every answer and says how to change one and how to go to the next card.

Each reply is a page of the card's history and belongs to the question on it. To change an answer, go back to its question with Control + Shift + up and send a new one: it replaces the old answer, and the card returns to where you were and says what changed. Control + Shift + down steps forward again.

With the first answer a keyboard shows at the foot of the card and stays for the rest of the onboarding. On the onboarding card's last reply it lights left Control, left Shift and up, then left Control, Shift and right. With the pointer over a key's name on a card, those keys light too; on 101: Hotkeys that is the only way its keys light.

The onboarding also makes a sample project, a folder named `facilitator-sample` in your home folder (an existing one is never touched), and opens it as your first project. While the last card shows, the board's three top-right buttons fade in, in their exact place on the board: the speaker, the bug and the settings squid (the onboarding's white page covers the board's own bar, so the page draws them, and the board's bar takes over with no jump when the board opens). The speaker and the squid are greyed and cannot be pressed; the bug is at full strength and opens the board's Share Feedback sheet. It works because your answers are saved as your profile as soon as the summary card ("Here is what you told us") is passed, which is what wakes the Share Feedback box (it is greyed, and the server refuses a send, until the profile has a name and an email); the board is told, so it keeps the onboarding up until the last card is done even though the profile now exists. A change to an answer is saved again when the summary card is passed again. The buttons are not shown on any card before the last. Marking the last card, "You are all set up", Done (Control + Shift + Backspace or its cross) saves your answers again and shows the sample project's board; with an answer missing it goes back to the first question not answered instead, and asks for that answer with the same short line (on the first question, the name, it shows the Tab hint again).

Your answers are your profile, kept on the Mac where only your account can read them, and changed in Settings, Profile on the Mac: a field is saved when you leave it, and giving an email there is the same yes as giving one in the onboarding, while clearing the email takes the yes back. What leaves the Mac is under [Usage counts](privacy.md#usage-counts).
