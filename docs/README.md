- [Using the board](board.md): How do the ticket list, the top bar, the home page, the keys, a card's magic box and the reply chime work? How do I browse and select cards?
- [Settings and the profile](settings.md): What is on the settings page? Which settings belong to the board? What does the first-run onboarding ask, and how do I change my profile?
- [Privacy and what the board keeps](privacy.md): What leaves my Mac, and what is the "Share daily usage counts" switch? Who can reach the board? What does the board log?
